# 40 staged encounters, 900 ticks each, rules version 3

One bold adult, one wolf already stalking her from three tiles, nothing else nearby. Three arms per seed.
"armed, defence off" is the `rich-no-defence` ablation: she still has the spear, she just will not use it.

| arm | drove off | failed | wolf left on its own | set out | bites taken | ended healthy | still stalked at the end | spear wear |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| armed, defence on | 18 | 22 | 0 | 40 | 0.55 | 100 | 0/40 | 3.6 |
| armed, defence off | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 100 | 0/40 | 0 |
| unarmed | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 100 | 0/40 | 0 |

Of 40 armed encounters with the action on, 40 set out, 40 reached the wolf and tried, 18 drove it off.
With the action off and the same spear in hand: 0 driven off, as it must be.

Bites are the honest comparison, and the numbers above are small: read them as "the mechanism runs and costs
what it costs", not as a survival benefit. Nothing here is a village.

## And in an ordinary village, left to itself

The same code, nothing staged: a 28-person world, no arrivals, twelve days. Run twice - once as it comes, and
once with every adult handed a spear on day 2, to separate "how often does this happen" from "does it work".

| village | spears | chances to stand | set out | reached and tried | drove off | wolf left on its own | deaths |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| as it comes | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| every adult armed on day 2 | 19 | 52 | 7 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 1 |

Read the first row as the honest autonomous rate: a village left to itself makes about one spear in twelve days,
because only somebody who has actually met a wolf wants one, and almost never has an armed adult and a wolf in
the same place at the same moment. The second row is what the behaviour does once the spears exist. Neither row
is evidence of a survival benefit, and the deaths column is far too small a number to read anything into.
