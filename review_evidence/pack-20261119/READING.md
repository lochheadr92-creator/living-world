# Step 3 gathering experiment

Use the already measured six 13-day rules-257 continuations with workshop access on. For each uncensored adult gather walk with a positive observed pack gain, report small loads, walking ticks per unit, and purpose. Distinguish eating at the source from gathering to carry stock. The historical design asks whether clearing spare material could fit the planned quantity in half the successful tiny gathers with room for at most two and a plan above two. Report this cohort literally and identify how the planner caps stocking amounts before departure.

Test an opportunistic preparation policy: at a known home, before a non-urgent stocking walk, put down spare materials if that enables a load of at least three. First screen reach with an optimistic upper bound: count every qualifying stocking departure within two tiles of home, assume all wood, stone, clay and seeds are free to deposit, enough shelf space and zero extra deposit travel. Compare that bound with all pack-limited small loads and the literal design cohort. If even this bound covers less than half of pack-limited small loads at every start, reject this policy as the proposed broad remedy. This additional threshold is a proposal for this candidate. The source design threshold concerns physical room and has a different denominator.

Replay the first day of each save to classify pack contents using actual actor-local needs, commitments and deposit rules at departure. Protect equipment, food, water, active errands and reserves. Report potential spare clay separately as an upper bound. A counterfactual room calculation measures feasibility only. If rejected, retain rules 257 and report baseline deliveries, hunger and survival without claiming changed outcomes.
