  HOW 256 IS COMPARED WITH 255 (fixed now)
  For each world, horizon and row, take the range at 256 and the range at 255 (spread_255_d12 and spread_255_d24, already committed).
  The row is NARROWER if the 256 range is smaller, WIDER if larger, TIED if equal. A row that is zero in all three arms at both
  versions is left out as absent. Reported per world and horizon: the number of rows narrower, wider, tied, and the same for the four
  yardstick shares alone and for people and deaths. This is a description of three values against three values and carries no
  significance statement; one row can go either way by chance (a range of three values is a loose estimate), so a count of rows is the
  reading, not any single row.
  Also reported, because 256 is a different world from 255 and not the same world with less noise: the mean of the three arms at 256
  against the mean at 255 for the same save, row by row, as a number only.

PART B. EQUIVALENCE FROM FRESH WORLDS, 255 AGAINST 256
  The paired lab the rules 192 to 196 checks used. No rule changed in 256, so the rows should sit inside the noise floor.
    earlier: node scripts\lab.mjs --out C:\dev\lw_runs\stage1c_eq255 --rules 255 --seeds 12 --fork 0 --days 25 --profile normal --branches nudge:1,nudge:2
    later:   node scripts\lab.mjs --out C:\dev\lw_runs\stage1c_eq256 --seeds 12 --fork 0 --days 25 --profile normal --branches nudge:1,nudge:2
    then:    npx vite-node scripts/lab_versions.ts -- C:\dev\lw_runs\stage1c_eq255 C:\dev\lw_runs\stage1c_eq256 <report>
  12 seeds, Normal, 25 days, arrivals on (the default), fork 0, control and two nudges, as in the earlier checks. Evidence is copied to
  review_evidence/stage1c-20261118/equivalence_255_256/.
  What counts as a move: the verdict of lab_versions.ts, "larger than chance", which needs the interval to exclude 0 and the mean
  difference to be at least the noise floor. A metric that is "larger than chance" is a finding to run down before this goes anywhere
  (it is not a stop, as the brief says). "Systematic, within the noise floor" and "not distinguishable from chance" are read as
  equivalence, with the weakness stats.ts states: a dozen seeds is weak evidence of no effect.
  A note on the floor: the lab's floor is how far one arm lands from the mean of the other arms of its run, pooled over both versions.
  Keyed draws make the 256 arms land closer together on what they key, so the pooled floor is smaller than 255 alone would give, and it
  is easier for a metric to pass it. If a metric passes only because of that, it is reported with the 255-only and 256-only floors beside
  it, computed from the same rows.
  Seeds whose control and nudge:1 end with the same hash at 25 days are counted and listed. Any such seed is a stop condition 1 event
  in the sense above.

  WHAT I EXPECT
  1. No metric is "larger than chance" (LIKELY). With about a hundred metrics, two or three may be, by chance, in which case I run
     them down by seed before calling them anything.
  2. Several metrics that depend on weather or arrival timing (hungry shares, thirst exposure, arrivals) show a smaller spread between
     arms at 256 than at 255 (LIKELY), and the mean of people differs by under one person (UNKNOWN).
  3. The first seed where the 256 and 255 controls differ does so at tick 50 or earlier in the weather channel, and every seed's two
     controls end with different hashes (LIKELY; checked on gen-4: first difference at tick 50).

WHAT THIS STEP CANNOT SAY
  - Part A: three worlds, three arms each, one run each. The range of three values is a loose estimate of a spread, and three worlds
    cannot be pooled.
  - Part A: the saves are rules 250 raised to 256, a reinterpretation for measurement, not a continuation of rules 250.
  - The narrowing of the spread is by construction (the weather and four other systems no longer move with a nudge). It says how much
    of the old noise sat in them. It is not evidence that the world got better, and no benefit is claimed.
  - The nudge at 256 shakes only what stays on the shared stream, so the spread at 256 is a spread of the births, deaths, wildlife and
    names remainder. A removal arm that moves a row by less than that remainder moves cannot be told from it.
  - Part B: 12 seeds is weak evidence of equivalence, and 25 days is not 240.
