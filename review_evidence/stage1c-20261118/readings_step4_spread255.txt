THE SPREAD
  For one world and one row: the three final values (control, nudge:1, nudge:2); the range = largest minus smallest; the mean.
  Reported per world, never pooled across the three worlds. Also the range as a share of the mean where the mean is not zero.

WHAT COUNTS AS A MOVE (used by step 6 and step 8, fixed here so it is not chosen after the numbers)
  An arm moved on a row in one world when its value lies outside [smallest - R, largest + R] of the three reference arms, where
  R = the range of the three, but never less than a floor of 1 for a count row and 0.5 percentage points for a share row (so a
  row that happened to tie in all three is not flagged for a one-unit difference).
  Why not just "outside the range": a fourth value from the very same distribution falls outside the range of three values half
  the time (checked by simulation, 2 million trials: normal 50.0%, exponential 50.0%). Outside by a full range: 13.3% normal,
  15.0% exponential. Outside by one and a half ranges: 8.1% and 10.9%. So with 26 rows, about three or four rows per world will
  look moved by chance at one full range, and that is expected, not a finding. A removal arm is read per world; a row counts as
  a finding only when it moves the same way in at least 4 of the 6 step 8 starts (chance for that at 13% per start is about 0.3%, and with 26 rows about 1 in 11
  that some row passes this test in one arm by chance, so even a finding is read together with the guard rails).
  The simulation script is C:\dev\lw_runs\profile\null_rate.mjs (not in git; it is twenty lines).

WHAT THIS STEP CANNOT SAY
  - Three worlds, three arms each, one run each: the range of three values is a loose estimate of the true spread (about 1.7
    standard deviations on average for a normal, and it can be far off in either direction).
  - Saves from rules 250 raised to 255 are reinterpreted for measurement. Arrivals on, with a purity check, is not tested.
  - The nudge shakes only what draws from the shared stream. At 255 that is everything random; at 256 it will not be.
  - Spread at 12 and 24 days is not the spread at 240 days.
