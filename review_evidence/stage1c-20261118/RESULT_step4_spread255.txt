THE MOVE BAND, CHECKED AGAINST THE EXTRA ARMS (not part of the readings; added in the dated note)
  The rule: an arm moved on a row when it lies outside [smallest - R, largest + R] of the three reference arms (floors 1 for counts, 0.5 pp for shares).
  Applied to nudge:3, 4 and 5 against the three references at 12 days (spread_255_d12_extra/analysis_extra.txt): 18 of 162 row cells fall outside,
  11 percent, against about 13 to 15 percent that the rule's own simulation says a fourth value from the same distribution falls outside by a full range.
  So the band is not too tight in aggregate. A cell by cell look shows some rows that the band would have called a move from noise alone, for example
  aspen plotsRestingAtEnd (references 4, 5, 4; extras 1, 8, 1), river workNotesKept (references 96, 95, 89; extras 95, 112, 120) and workTakenUp (44 to 46; extras 45, 56, 64),
  aspen people (references 60 to 61; extras 59, 56, 58). A removal arm that moves such a row by that much is not a finding on its own, and the file's
  rule of 4 of 6 starts for a finding stands.
