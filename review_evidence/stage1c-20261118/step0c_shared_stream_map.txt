WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE NUDGE
A nudge draws N numbers before step 1. Anything still on world.rng after rules 256 (births, deaths, wildlife, relocation names and
the births' createPerson draws) is still shifted by it. So a nudge stays a valid noise probe, but it shakes only what is left on the
shared stream. The report must say that, and say that the weather, arrivals, spoilage, trees and grain would no longer move with it.
