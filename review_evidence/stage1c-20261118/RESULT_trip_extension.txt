What this does and does not say
  Staleness is not what sends people to stores they cannot take from: the belief behind nearly every walk was under a day old.
  The allowance is zero with stock present on 42 to 75% of these walks. The observer does not separate why (an earmark held for someone
  else, no access, a granary share). The earlier trip waste report attributed it to earmarks; this run does not re-verify that.
  CONFLICT to settle, not settled: the trip waste report gave 29 to 40% of adult withdraws arriving and taking nothing. This read gives
  64% (river, 36 of 56), 71% (aspen, 94 of 132) and 88% (gen-4, 116 of 132) of the walks that were read and whose activity count is known
  taking nothing. LIKELY reason: this counts walks,
  and a withdraw that begins beside the store has no walk, so the successful near ones are missing from the denominator. UNKNOWN until
  the two are put on one definition. Do not quote either figure as the share of all withdraws.
