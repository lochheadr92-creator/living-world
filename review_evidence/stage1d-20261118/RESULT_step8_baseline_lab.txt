5. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
  - 13 days, three seeds, two ages. A part whose effect builds over years (trees, a reserve over several winters, field fertility) can
    show as a null here and matter later. The two ages are one settlement each seed (aspen day 48 and day 96 are the same village), so six
    starts are three settlements. That is why the per-seed lines are reported, and why store food and no-food hunger, which agree in both
    starts of all three seeds, are the strongest rows.
  - The reference is loose. In gen-4 day 48 and river day 48 nudge:2 ended with the control's hash, so those spreads rest on two
    trajectories. Across the six reference arms the distinct trajectories are 3, 5, 4, 6, 4 and 5 (aspen 48, aspen 96, gen-4 48, gen-4 96,
    river 48, river 96): 27 of 36. At 256 a nudge reaches only the shared stream (births, old age, wildlife, names), and many nudges realign.
    A move smaller than that remainder cannot be told from it. The six-arm check exists for this. It dropped one finding (walking share), weakened two
    (no-food hunger from 6 of 6 to 5, hungry share from 5 to 4) and removed the one death signal. It kept the other findings.
  - A removal arm removes the part going forward only, from the save's day on.
  - This lab compares 256 with a part taken out. It does not compare 255 with 256 (that was step 6, read as equivalence over 24 and 25 days).
  - The yardstick figure of 15 to 18% in the plan was not reproduced (11 to 13% here, 10 to 12% at 255 in step 4). What it was measured
    on is UNKNOWN, so the 30% target is being read against a baseline of about 12%, not 15 to 18%.
  - hungryNoFoodShare is a provisional cell. If the choice of cell changes, the hunger rows change with it.

6. ONE LINE ON WHETHER 255 STAYS THE DEFAULT (Ryan decides; nothing is changed)
  Leave 255 as it is and take no rules action from this step. None of the four parts 252 to 255 worsens deaths or no-food hunger on these
  six starts, the grow-more part (254) is the one whose removal leaves stores lower and no-food hunger higher in every seed, and the
  farm draw (255) does what it was built to do on plots rested. The evidence is 13 days. Moving the default to 256 is a separate choice that step 6 supports on equivalence for
  24 to 25 days, and nothing here argues against it.
  What would change this: a rerun of the grow-more removal on fresh seeds at day 96 that does not repeat the store food fall, or a part
  whose removal lowers no-food hunger in 4 or more starts (none did).
