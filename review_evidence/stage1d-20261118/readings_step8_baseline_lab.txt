THE REFERENCE SPREAD
  For one save and one row: the values of control, nudge:1 and nudge:2; the range R0 = largest minus smallest; the mean of the three.
  Each removal arm is compared with that spread, never with one control alone (AGENTS.md gotchas).

WHAT COUNTS AS A MOVE (the rule fixed in readings_step4_spread255.txt, copied here and not changed)
  A removal arm moved on a row in one save when its value lies outside [smallest - R, largest + R] of the three reference arms,
  where R = the range of the three, never less than a floor of 1 for a count row and 0.5 percentage points for a share row.
  Reported per row and arm as "moved beyond the spread in n of 6 starts", with the direction of each move (up or down).
  A FINDING is a row that moves the same way in at least 4 of the 6 starts. Anything less is not a finding.
  Chance reading, so a finding is not over-read: a fourth value from the same distribution lies outside the range by a full range
  about 13% of the time (checked by simulation, step 4). Four or more of six in one direction then has a chance of well under 1%
  per row, and with about 30 rows per arm about 1 arm in 10 shows a false finding on some row. So a finding is read with the guard
  rails and the size of the move, and a lone row is a lead, not a result.
  The six starts are not six independent worlds: day 48 and day 96 of one seed are the same settlement a year and a half apart.
  So each finding is also reported per seed: whether both of that seed's starts moved the same way. A finding that rests on one
  seed's two starts is called that.

GUARD RAILS (plan line 159, applied to the removal arms)
  Deaths and hungry time with no food to hand stay inside the spread of the nudged controls. Read here as: a removal arm that moves
  deaths or hungryNoFoodShare DOWN (fewer deaths, less hungry time without food) says the part makes it worse; UP says the part helps.
  Both directions are reported for every start. The yardstick target is 30% company plus leisure. The plan's figure of 15 to 18% at
  rules 250 was not reproduced (about 10 to 12% on the day 48 saves at 255, step 4), so what the plan measured is itself unclear.

WHAT THIS STEP CANNOT SAY
  - Three worlds at two ages and 13 days is not 240 days. A part whose effect builds over years (trees, a bigger reserve over several
    winters, field fertility) can show as a null here and matter later.
  - Three reference arms give a loose spread. A removal arm that moves by less than the shared-stream remainder cannot be told from it.
  - At 256 a nudge reaches only the shared stream remainder (births, old age, wildlife, names): weather, arrivals, spoilage, trees
    and grain are keyed and do not move with a nudge. The spread is therefore smaller than at 255 by construction and a removal arm
    is judged against a tighter yardstick. That makes a move easier to see and no more meaningful by itself.
  - A removal arm from a save removes the part going forward only.
  - Whether 255 stays the default is Ryan's decision.
