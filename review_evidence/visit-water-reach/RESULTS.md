# How often direct drinking came up during ordinary welfare visits

All residents were eligible in three day-96 to day-97 replays per settlement. The only eligible direct drink was Ulric offering water to Pavel at tick 232224. Pavel went from 37.92 to 73.92 hydration, Ulric consumed one water from his pack, and the visit succeeded. The previously tested later Pavel encounter did not recur as a second drink on this trajectory.

In the doorway-only runs, 23 visits reached the needs check. Only one involved a thirsty recipient and an offer backed by water in the visitor’s pack. It was also the only one blocked by carrying capacity. No other household provided a replication in this window. The direct-drink and doorway-only hashes were identical in Aspen and River. In Gen-4 both hashes reproduced the corresponding earlier first-Pavel experiment. These observations describe one day per world.

Doorways had a separate effect in Aspen. The native trajectory recorded one helped visit and five visits failing across different spaces. The doorway trajectory recorded three helped visits, zero different-space failures and one visit finding the remembered room empty after admission. It also recorded six failed searches for Mona II, compared with five on the native timeline. River’s four different-space failures persisted in every arm. Aggregate counts on different trajectories do not pair individual visits.
