## Strongest conclusions, by impact

1. **Ordinary worlds at current rules are not viable in the forest seed, and the earliest broken transition is in the choice stage, not the environment.** Agent F's 60,000-tick (25-day) ordinary forest run at rules 115 ends with 0 of 31 alive; the retained rules-114 forest ends with 0 alive at day 45, rules 113 with 5/27 dead, rules 107 with 4/27. Cause: `knowledge.ts:179-188 estimatedAmount` extrapolates regrowth at nominal pace and ignores `regrowRate(world)` (lean 0.1, winter down to 0.4), although the lean season is announced to everyone (`hardship.ts:72`) and winter is deterministic. The code path is ungated, so rules 115 shares it. (E1, P1.)
2. **The product foundation "Nobody consults the world's true state to plan" is contradicted at rules 115 in six decision paths** (agent D, all reproduced by test): welfare visits vanish the tick the subject dies anywhere and are judged on live health (`welfare.ts:129,43,138`); the household "dependants" set is live health of unseen members and feeds food-keeping arithmetic (`optutil.ts:156`, `options_survival.ts:269`); "enough" refusals read the site's true ledger (`social.ts:443-453`); empty-home claims read the true owner (`options_work.ts:1862`); proposing a household reads the other person's private affinity (`options_social.ts:577-582`); batch bookkeeping reads remote carts/sites (`batch_plan.ts:248-253`). 3. **The cart chain cannot complete in an ordinary world for code reasons, not only tuning.** Agent F: the project "acquire" offer subtracts carried inputs twice (`production.ts:1195` passes the remaining quantity into `supply`, which subtracts the carried amount again at `:514-517`), so a hammer-less planner holding one stone is never offered the second (the retained rules-113 trace spent 104/105 "acquire stone" samples in exactly that state); plan reservations are netted without adoption order (`reserved_goods.ts:3-12` vs `intent.ts shareOf`), so a sibling tool plan can zero the batch's own held inputs; workshop planning counts shelf stock the person's access level cannot use (`production.ts:376-383` vs `facilities.ts:99-107`), producing 137 failed operate starts in 25 river days. ### E1 — Remembered-source estimates ignore the announced lean season and winter (P1, systematic failure, high confidence, T+R+S)
- Refs: `src/sim/knowledge.ts:179-188`; real regrowth `src/sim/sources.ts:100` multiplies by `regrowRate(world)` (`hardship.ts:138`: lean 0.1, dry, winter 1−0.6·sin); announcement `hardship.ts:72` (`think(...,'lean',...)` to every living person). Consumers `optutil.ts:287-293`, `options_survival.ts:163-174`, `relief.ts:62`, `food_help.ts:31`.
- Trigger: any lean season (1-in-5 per day after day 2) or winter (days 8–11 of every 12-day year), sources shared by many.
- Expected: `knowledge.ts:178` "People understand roughly how fast things regrow"; PRODUCT_VISION "Local knowledge and plausible inhabitant decisions". Observed: 53/184 trips doomed at departure, 7 abandonments of verified near food for believed far food, 64 "something better came up" travel interruptions, intake 93 units/day for 29 people; the same day with the lean flag removed (diagnostic intervention, not a rules change): 4/139 doomed, 148 units got, mean hunger 47→62 instead of 47→44. Retained `food107-forest-days18-24`: 1,340 gather starts, 654 interrupted "something better came up", 104 successes, 63% of living ticks travelling.
- Repro: `tests/zz_review_e_famine_trace.test.ts` (rules-107 save, ungated path).
- Earliest broken transition: retained knowledge → eligible/chosen decision. Why missed: observers counted "known source estimated nonempty" as a food offer without comparing to the real amount at departure.
- Consequence: scarcity becomes travel churn; ~85–170 units left standing while people starve. Limits: the famine is also environmental (lean × winter regrowth 4–10% cannot feed 29 people); the intervention bounds, not isolates, the belief effect.
- Fix: scale the extrapolated growth in `estimatedAmount` by the pace the person has been told about (`regrowRate`, or at least `winterDepth`); version-gated; regression: lean world, source seen empty N ticks ago → estimate ≤ true regrowth; staged near-verified vs far-believed choice.
