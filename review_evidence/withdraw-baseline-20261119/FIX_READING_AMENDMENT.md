# Safety reading clarification

Written after launch and before any 13-day altered-run output exists. The earlier reading's phrase “hungry waking time with no food in pack” is incomplete for the historical noise comparison: the lab's `hungryNoFoodShare` counts waking time hungry, with no food in the pack **and** no food in the household home store (including no home). Calculate it from the DayShape cross-tab by summing `packFood=none` and `homeStoreFood=empty` or `no_home`, divided by all waking ticks. Use the historical maximum absolute control-to-nudge difference, in percentage points: aspen 48 0.92, aspen 96 0.59, gen-4 48 0.66, gen-4 96 1.19, river 48 1.40, river 96 1.68. These historical figures are rounded to two decimal places; describe a result near a boundary as inconclusive rather than manufacturing precision. The control in the survival comparison is the same-save 257 switch-off arm. The historical spread is a practical reference, not a fresh measurement of 257 variability.
