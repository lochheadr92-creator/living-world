# Rules 256 withdrawal baseline: reading fixed before the runs

Continue the six rules-256 saves (aspen, river, gen-4 at days 48 and 96) for 13 days each, immigration on, without changing rules. Record one control per save and compare its final hash with the existing control for that save. The two saves from a seed are not independent settlements. No claim of a population rate or benefit is made.

Count completed adult withdrawal activities by whether the sampled activity had any travel ticks, and all completed withdrawals. Separately report empty, other zero-take, successful/partial and interrupted activities; the finished-only denominator excludes censored activities, whose count is reported independently. A walk-based percentage and an all-attempt percentage have different denominators; the difference alone does not prove the historical discrepancy is fully resolved. Activities already in progress at the first tick are not fresh departures; first-phase is retained and reported.

Retain stock and access at the last sampled work tick, classify the cause only when observed, and distinguish stock earmarked to another person from other reasons for a zero allowance. Record the observer's unseen ends, censored activities, arrivals, resource conservation and end hashes. A matching end hash against the previous control is a sampled deterministic check, not a proof on all worlds.

Existing time and trip observers retain their own definitions. Baseline is descriptive; comparisons and thresholds for any change must be fixed before the altered runs. The previously recorded rules-250 empty-withdrawal figures and rules-256 baseline are historical reference, not a guaranteed present value.
