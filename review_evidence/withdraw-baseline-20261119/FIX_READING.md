# Workshop access comparison

Fixed before the altered runs. Starting from each of the six rules-256 saves, run 13 days at rules 257 with workshop access on and with `setBenchAccess(false)`, one of each per save, arrivals on. This is an exploratory paired comparison on three settlements at two ages, not six independent seeds and not a population rate. The two nudges in the existing 256 study describe noise at 256, not a formal confidence interval for this new rule.

Primary mechanism: among finished adult withdrawals, compare the fraction with result `took_none_empty`; separately show the fraction among those observed in the work phase, finished-with-walking, and the stock-present/zero-allowance subset. The draft's original proposed benchmark is a reduction by at least half in every start; it is an exacting diagnostic target, not a guarantee. Count incomplete and unseen endings, and report a failed observer check rather than silently dropping it. Show absolute counts as well as shares.

Safety: compare deaths, hungry waking time with no food in pack, end store food per living person, and resource conservation. As in the earlier reading, treat deaths above control plus two, or hungry-with-no-food percentage points above control plus the historical 256 nudge spread plus three, as survival not holding. A pass at 13 days does not prove longer-term survival. Show the source of each noise number and mark unavailable spreads unknown. Read the paths affected by recipe inputs and smithy wants and do not attribute a world-wide outcome to collection alone. Compare total waking company plus leisure and total travel time descriptively; adult-stage travel has the reported stage self-check limitation.

An observer mismatch, failed conservation, incompatible saves, failed old-version checks, or an unverified causal path is a limitation or failure, not evidence of benefit. Keep the concurrent draft's provenance and report any deviation from it. No promotion, merge or push follows automatically from this comparison.
