# Interpretation limits checked during analysis

- The six worlds are a fixed descriptive sample, not random independent human-like subjects or a controlled causal test of map size or age. Normal and Large differ in population, map size, camps and generated layouts. Founder cohorts are grouped by initial stage; actual stage changes are retained separately.
- The earlier five-day report was pinned to a rules-155 candidate. This investigation is pinned to main commit 79e3ac402ebd93812c93b53c7947e406649dbafe, rules 153. Do not label differences from that earlier report as a measured improvement or regression.
- Activity time includes travel and waiting while the activity is selected. A warm-activity percentage is not necessarily time physically standing at a fire.
- `lessons` counts participation in successfully completed foraging practice. It does not alone distinguish teaching from learning. Inspect participant age, skills and local observations for individual claims.
- The exact message `they had no room to carry it` is a reported handover failure, not an independently checked inventory diagnosis for every counted case. Frozen `src/sim/social.ts` returns it after zero goods move; non-resident-care modes can also reach that branch. The observer did not snapshot both inventories at every failure. Preserve precise local evidence where available and do not call all counted failures proven capacity failures or causes of death. The broader `roomFailures` dictionary also includes unrelated messages containing the word `room`; do not sum it as a capacity metric.
- A successful deposit proves goods reached storage. It does not prove the intended recipient later ate those particular goods. A fallback selected after a failed handover can itself be interrupted.
- The simulation's recorded death cause is retained, but preceding cold, hunger, exhaustion and illness can coexist. A final cause string does not establish the entire causal chain.
- Existing raw death records round the recorded age; exact age at death can be derived from initial age plus death tick / 28800. Some raw final-stage fields use the world's end time for dead people; use death-time age, not those fields, when classifying a death.
- `summary.json` and the charts include only fully completed worlds at the time they are generated. Final reporting must verify the five original reports, original driver completion and the separately labelled supplemental report before claiming that all six requested world outcomes are available. The failed original remains a failure.
- A postprocessing read initially failed because Windows defaulted to cp1252. Explicit UTF-8 fixed it; no simulation was rerun or changed for that error. A PowerShell inspection also had an unquoted numeric property syntax error; it was corrected without changing data.

- Death handling clears the live person object inventory. Raw final inventory/capacity for a deceased person must not be read as their inventory/capacity just before death. Use the retained death-account pack and last living daily sample. For example, Kai made a basket at tick 10360, had capacity 12 from the day-5 sample onward and retained the basket in his death-account pack; the raw post-death final capacity of six is not evidence that he lacked a basket. The summary now labels last-living-sample capacity and leaves deceased finalCapacity null.
