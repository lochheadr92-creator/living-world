Before running: compare twelve days from fresh Normal Aspen and River starts, arrivals on, on the working rules-266 branch. Measure old wild and field yields (base), the same field yield with current wild grain (wild), current wild with ripe-plot targets of 18 and 24 (t18, t24), and the current combined boost (boost). Also replay the two archived rules-261 Aspen arms for all twelve daily hashes. No product setting changes.

The underlying seven-point crop scale is floor((3 + round(4 × accumulated care)) × (0.5 + 0.5 × soil condition)), at least one. Current pre-boost stock is three times that scale; maximum 21. The present boost adds floor(half), maximum 31. For the trial targets use max(1, floor(scale × target / 7)), keeping seed yield, growing time, labour, soil, and collection mechanics. Compare dose only with the same wild setting. At full care and healthy soil 18 and 24 are alternatives either side of 21. Each field pays its ordinary planting seed cost.

Report created, harvested, gathered, deposited (gross movement), ending stores, human eating, processing, animal feeding, seed saving and spoilage. Track flour, bread and prepared feed separately to avoid treating processed inputs as meals. Grain identity disappears when stacks merge. Interpret on-minus-off as a difference between whole histories; never assign a pooled grain to a particular eater. Check opening + created − consumed − spoiled = closing, and field ripening = harvested + rot + change in standing crop. Report partial and repeat harvests of the same ripening. Archive per-kind ledger reasons and transfer routes.
