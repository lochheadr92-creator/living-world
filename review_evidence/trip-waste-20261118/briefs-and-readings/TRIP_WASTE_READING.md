Written before the new runs start and before any trip-waste table of this task exists. Its SHA-256 is recorded in `out\trip-waste\commands.txt` at launch and it is not edited afterwards. The thresholds below are mine (Ryan to veto), as the thresholds of the earlier readings were mine.

## What is measured
Rules 250, the three day 48 saves (aspen, river, gen-4), 30 days, one run per world, arm A (gate off, no flags). Three new runs, one wave. They are the A runs again with one more read-only observer attached (`scripts/observers/withdraw_check.ts`, flag `--withdraw-check` of `scripts/free_probe.ts`). Controls: the A, N1 and N2 runs already in `out\free`, not rerun. Gate off and a read-only observer must leave the world as it was, so each new run must end on the hash of its A control: `168d98f7` (aspen), `8f513a37` (river), `523f2943` (gen-4). If one differs, the observer is not read-only and nothing from that run is used.

Three questions, each with its own data:

1. **Loads (gather trips, people of 12 and over).** From the `trips.jsonl` of the nine existing runs (A, N1, N2 in three worlds; no new run). For a gather walk that did its job: units taken, the room the pack had at the start of the walk for the item mainly taken (`floor((cap - pack weight) / item weight)`, `economy.ts roomFor`), the units planned. A gather is **room-limited** when units taken equal that room and the room is below the planned units; **plan-limited** when units taken equal the planned units and the plan is below the room; **source-limited** when units taken are below both; **other** otherwise (a tie or an unknown plan). Also what the pack held at the start (share of the carrying capacity in weight, split into food, wood, stone and other materials, tools, seed, other).
2. **Withdraws (people of 12 and over).** From the new runs. For every withdraw: the person's belief about the place (units of the wanted kinds, how old, seen or told), the true units there at departure, at the first tick of work, and at the last tick before the transfer, the units wanted and taken, the pack room, the store's class (own household, shared, another household, a pile or site), and how the trip ended. A withdraw **took none, empty** when it reached work and ended "there was nothing to take". Of those: **stale at departure** when the true units of the wanted kinds were 0 when the person set out; **drained on the way** when they were above 0 at departure and 0 on arrival; **emptied while waiting** when above 0 on arrival and 0 at the last tick before the transfer; **stock there, nothing allowed** when the true units were above 0 at the last tick before the transfer. A belief is a **glance** when every wanted kind the person believes is there is believed at 1 or less and the true units of the main kind are 2 or more (the signature of rules 169, `seen_stock.ts`).
3. **Dropped walks (all kinds that haul).** From the `trips.jsonl` of the nine runs. The walks counted `switched_mid_walk` or `gave_up_before_arrival`: the sim's own reason string, the kind switched to and the walking ticks, by kind (gather, withdraw, deposit, haul, fetch_water, harvest). The reason "the place looked empty" is the found-empty rule (rules 161, `found_empty.ts`).

## How it is read (my proposals; Ryan to veto)
- **TINY LOADS ARE ROOM** when, in at least 2 of 3 worlds, at least half of the gather walks that did their job and took 2 units or fewer were room-limited. **TINY LOADS ARE THE PLAN** when at least half were plan-limited. **TINY LOADS ARE THE SOURCE** when at least half were source-limited. Anything else is **MIXED**. Read from the pooled A, N1 and N2 runs of each world.
- **EMPTY WITHDRAWS ARE STALE BELIEF** when, in at least 2 of 3 worlds, at least half of the withdraws that took none, empty, were stale at departure with a belief of one or more units (the person set out for stock that was not there). **EMPTY WITHDRAWS ARE A RACE** when at least half were drained on the way or emptied while waiting. **EMPTY WITHDRAWS ARE ACCESS** when at least half had stock there and nothing allowed. Else **MIXED**.
- **GLANCE** is reported as a share of withdraws and of the empty ones; there is no threshold, the figure is the finding.
- **DROPPED WALKS ARE FOUND-EMPTY** when, for gather and withdraw together, "the place looked empty" is at least half the walking ticks of walks that ended before arrival, in at least 2 of 3 worlds; **ARE REVIEW SWITCHES** when "something better came up" and "switched to something else" together are at least half; else **MIXED**.
- Walking ticks lost: for each of the three questions the walking ticks of the walks counted as waste (a withdraw that took none, a gather that took 2 units or fewer, a walk dropped before arrival) as a share of all walking ticks of people of 12 and over, per world.

## Limits stated now
One run per arm and world: a description, never a rate. The A, N1 and N2 controls give a spread, not a test. Rules 250, not 255, and young worlds (day 48 saves, 51 to 55 people). Questions 1 and 3 read nine runs; question 2 reads three. The belief is read at the first sample after the activity began, which can be one tick after the choice. A withdraw whose activity ends before a sample sees it is counted and left out of the classes. No rule, score, pin, lab arm or file under docs/ changes; scratch only.
