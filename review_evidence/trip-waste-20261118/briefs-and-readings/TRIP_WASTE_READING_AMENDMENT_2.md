# Trip waste reading, amendment 2 (SCRATCH ONLY, 18 November 2026, written before the second wave starts)

`TRIP_WASTE_READING.md` (SHA-256 `c223694b8482d55675cb174633956f6d52b033d20fab677e33b2a5625dadc47d`) and amendment 1 (`ad35c7f96e529a1569d96a184fed1feef7b014574ba09a595cff8e7db09b897f`) are not edited. The first wave (`out\trip-waste`, three runs, ended 00:42) answered question 2 as **EMPTY WITHDRAWS ARE ACCESS**: 94 to 95% of the withdraws that took none arrived at a place that held the wanted goods and were allowed none. That wave could not say why, because the observer recorded only the place's stock, not who the stock was set aside for. This amendment adds the one measurement that does, in a second wave, before it starts.

## Source read that prompted it (VERIFIED by reading, 18 November, scratch tree at rules 250)
- `production.ts collectOptions` plans a collect from a workshop from `site_supply.ts benchStock`, which since rules 229 counts the workshop's own record of its output waiting for pickup (`b.ops.output`) as stock for anybody who may use the workshop, whoever ordered the batch (`b.ops.client` is in the snapshot and is not tested there, except to count a batch of the person's own that will be ready on arrival).
- `act_resource.ts` withdraw `work()` takes goods through `facilities.ts withdrawAllowance`: at a granary only the household's own share (`ops.shares`, plus a small ration if the person is starving or has a starving dependant); at a workplace `usableUnits`: stock earmarked to the person, plus stock that is not earmarked when the person's access is owner, communal or builder. Finished goods are earmarked (`kind: 'out'`, `owner: client`) to whoever ordered the batch.
So a person who is not the client sees bread waiting at the bakery, plans a collect, and is allowed none. The first wave's numbers fit this (bakery bread withdraws: aspen 876, 53 took, 665 empty; mean believed 0.99, mean true 4.18) but do not show it.

## What the second wave adds
The same three runs again (arm A, gate off, `--withdraw-check`, 30 days, one wave), from the same day 48 saves, with `withdraw_check.ts` extended to record, at the last work-phase sample before the transfer and with the sim's own pure functions (`accessLevel`, `withdrawAllowance` of `facilities.ts`, called read-only): the allowance the transfer would give for the wanted kinds, the units in the store, the units set aside by a live earmark (`until` above the tick, `n` above 0) in total, to the person, and to other members of the person's own household, the person's access level, and the household's granary share. At departure also: whether the person's own belief of the workshop names them as the client of the running batch (`belief.ops.client`), and the output units the belief says wait. Each run must end on the hash of its A control (`168d98f7`, `8f513a37`, `523f2943`), and its withdraw counts and empty counts must equal the first wave's (same world, same code path); if not, the observer is not read-only and nothing from the second wave is used.

For each withdraw that took none, empty, with stock there and nothing allowed, the reason is, in this order: **granary, no share** (a granary and the household's share of the wanted kinds is 0); **no access** (access level none); **earmarked to others** (the units set aside to other people are at least the units in the store, and none are set aside to the person); **guest stock** (access level friend, no units set aside to the person, and some units not set aside to anyone); **other**. Of the earmarked to others: how many had an owner in the person's own household.

## How it is read (my proposals; Ryan to veto)
- **ACCESS IS EARMARK** when, in at least 2 of 3 worlds, at least half of the withdraws that took none, with stock there and nothing allowed, were earmarked to others. **ACCESS IS THE GRANARY SHARE** when at least half were granary, no share. **ACCESS IS GUEST STOCK** when at least half were guest stock. Else **MIXED**.
- Walking ticks of the earmarked-to-others withdraws as a share of all adult walking ticks, per world.
- The share of the earmarked-to-others whose owner is in the person's own household (reported, no threshold).
- The share of empty withdraws whose belief named the person as the client of the running batch (reported, no threshold).

## Limits
One run per world; the earmark state is read once per work tick and the last reading before the end is used, which can be up to a few ticks before the transfer; a withdraw at a pile or site has no earmarks and is counted `other`. This measures what the sim's access rules do, not whether they should. No change to any rule.
