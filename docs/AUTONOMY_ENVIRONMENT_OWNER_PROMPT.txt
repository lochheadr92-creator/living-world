Living World Autonomy and Environment Expansion Prompt
Audited against main on 30 October 2026
Implement the remaining inhabitant and environment capabilities in lochheadr92-creator/living-world. Give people the practical means to turn their own needs, knowledge, relationships and intentions into sustained physical action. Let the environment change through use, neglect, weather and recovery. Complete the connected experience: a viewer can follow a need, understand the choice, watch the work, see its consequence and recognise its effect on a later decision.

Preserve locomotion, perception, physical transfers, reservations, ownership, survival actions, save/replay and the existing action validator. Do not weaken these constraints to give inhabitants more freedom. Freedom comes from additional feasible choices, better coordination and practical preparation.
Keep the engine authoritative. Inhabitants act from their own state, observations and communicated information. Separate a true world fact from a belief, forecast or rumour. An absent person, depleted remote store or hidden danger cannot become known through a planning shortcut.
Every new capability needs a locally knowable trigger, prerequisites, a legal option, physical execution, time and material costs, interruption handling, a truthful outcome, saved state where necessary and a useful inspector explanation. If it requires a missing tool, container, procedure, permission, route or meeting, implement that prerequisite through the same physical rules.
Preserve deterministic replay. Use the existing seeded randomness and ordering conventions. Observation must not consume randomness or mutate simulation state. Record external decisions as inputs. Replaying an AI-controlled inhabitant uses the recorded transcript rather than calling a model again.
Account for every material source, transformation, transfer, loss and consumption. Promised goods are not inventory. Reserved goods cannot be spent twice. Distinguish contribution, ownership and permission. Release claims on cancellation, death, interruption and invalidation according to the existing contract.
