## Selected contract (recorded before implementation)

Two uses of one small capability: a person's remembered, accessible **raw material
stock** can support (1) a private/building material plan and (2) a material delivery
promise. Current natural-source searches ignore stored wood/stone/clay/ore; plan
reconsideration calls stored material unavailable, and helpers cannot promise to
fetch it. A completed storehouse and its actual deposits therefore lack these consumers.

The primitive is a pure query over existing building beliefs, limited to public
storehouses and the person's own household home. No remote stock lookup, workshop
earmark inference, new knowledge, inventory, receipt or persisted schema. Knowledge
owns the remembered quantity; production owns offered collection; existing resource
actions validate and transfer at physical contact; requests/commitments own delivery
and termination; relationships and current helper choice own feedback. An empty,
destroyed, inaccessible or changed store can still defeat an attempt.

Before: these consumers require a natural deposit even when known storage holds the
material. After: ordinary collection competes with gathering at existing utility,
travel and danger costs; useful known stock keeps a plan viable and can make a
voluntary promise feasible. Only actual delivery earns existing help/trust credit;
later helper choice can read that trust. No scores, capacities, deadlines or refusal
rules are relaxed. Both connections have isolated lab switches; old rules are gated.

Expected files: a small sim material-stock query; intent/social/options_work/production
call sites; withdrawal validation for the new offers; rulesversion and lab switches;
contract/continuation tests; evidence runners and this record/register/runbook.

## Primitive, lifecycle and version boundary

The missing capability is an authoritative **query of remembered accessible raw
stock** shared by material consumers. Existing beliefs, commitments, activities and
receipts already represent all required state; another persistence structure would
duplicate them. `material_stock.ts` owns this narrow interpretation, not logistics.

Observation or existing communication creates/updates a belief. The plan owner and
the answering helper can query only their own beliefs. At most two nearest known
stores add collection options, in stable order, using the existing collection cost,
travel, danger, weather and night calculations. Remembered quantity and carrying room
cap the offered amount. Nothing reserves or transfers goods by being considered.

The ordinary withdrawal travels to the place, observes it, rechecks own/public access
at both arrival and transfer, applies `withdrawAllowance` and capacity, then moves only
real units. Another person's home or a place whose ownership changed is rejected.
Depletion can yield a partial transfer or failure; disappearance is learned at arrival.
Interrupting before transfer grants nothing. Existing failures suppress immediate
retries. Requests retain ordinary refusal, deadline, cancellation and competing-need
rules. Only a real handover fulfils the promise and earns existing outcome credit.

Rules **40** gates both producers/consumers. `rich-no-stored-material-plans` removes
personal/building collection and viability; `rich-no-stored-material-promises` removes
promise feasibility and acquisition. They restore their previous switch value, do
not mutate saved settings, and have no effect through rules 39. New collection
activities carry one `materialStock` item marker in existing serialized activity data
so changed ownership is checked during execution. No save-format, observation or
protocol change: format 4, observation 12, protocols 21/22 retain their contracts.
