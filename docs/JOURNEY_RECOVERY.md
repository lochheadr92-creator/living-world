## Implemented owner and limits

`journey_recovery.ts` derives a replacement from at most 32 already legal scoped gather offers. It matches the material,
purpose category and, for material acquisition serving a construction site, the dominant site recorded by the existing
material-demand merger. This adds semantic context to the offer; it does not change how the merger chooses its demand or
how much physical material is required. A replacement requires an actual recorded failed gather at the current destination.
It excludes failed sources, allows at most two changes and waits at least 120 ticks between changes. It does not react to a
new attractive source when no failure occurred. No route search, unknown live-stock query or additional action handler is added.

Decision-time intention review records old place, new place, tick and personal cause, preserving the original two-day
deadline, supplied goods and prior history. The exact original source can remain blocked while other personally remembered
stock is positive; urgent needs may be met before another work step becomes legal. When no personally remembered positive
source remains, or replacement/failure bounds are exhausted, the normal intention settlement releases private claims and
records abandonment. Positive work still closes the trip, not the whole construction project. Lab removal:
`rich-no-journey-recovery`.

The current rules also reconcile private provision claims after actual portable consumption, so water already drunk is not
reported as missing future supply. This has no effect at rules 72. The inspector retains the most recent ended recovery trip
for 600 ticks beside a new active plan, so its result is not immediately hidden by the ordinary night plan. It shows actual
stored reasons; read-only inspection cannot create a change of source, consumed goods or a settlement.
