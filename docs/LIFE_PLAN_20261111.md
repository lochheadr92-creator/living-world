# Plan: letting the ordinary mechanisms fire (draft, 11 November 2026)

A plan drafted for the owner from `docs/MECHANISM_CENSUS_20261111.md`. Nothing here is approved or started by this document. It follows the repository's
conventions as they stand: one rules version per behaviour change, a module switch, contract tests with the previous-version contrast, an immutable continuation
pin from a clean checkout, a twin, the paired lab with the life metrics of `scripts/lab/lab.ts`, a record in `docs/`, a checkpoint paragraph in `AGENTS.md`, and
the Brain checkpoint. No pull requests, no second reviewer (`WORKFLOW.md`).

## Ground rules for the whole plan

- **Stream and numbers.** The balance stream's claim (195 to 199) has three numbers left. This plan claims a new stream, **life, rules 200 to 210**, in
  `docs/RULES_RANGES.md` before the first gated line is written, and leaves 197 to 199 unused rather than splitting one plan across two claims.
- **What counts as success, per item.** In the paired lab (12 seeds, Normal, 25 days, arrivals on, the previous version pinned against the new one) the item's own
  measure moves beyond the noise floor in the direction claimed, and none of people, deaths, hunger exposure, cold exposure or the work share moves against it beyond
  the floor. The full suite from the clean checkout shows no new failure. An item that does not clear its floor is recorded as a null and kept only if it closes a
  defect; otherwise its switch stays off by default and the record says so.
- **What is never done here.** No score boost to a social option "to make it visible"; every change below is a gate, an access rule or a cost, measured by whether
  the behaviour then happens on its own. The critical-need filter, the relief guard, danger, actor-local knowledge, conservation and save semantics are untouched.
  A change that would need a new durable field on the save is called out as such and costs a save-format note.
- **Order of landing.** The order below is by expected effect against size. Items 1, 3, 4 and 6 are gate changes of a few lines; they can be **screened in one lab
  run** first (each behind a lab switch on the same rules version, as the metabolism candidates were), then landed one at a time in the order the screening
  shows. Items 2 and 5 touch access and cooperation and get a short design note each before code. Items 7 and 8 are small. Item 9 is a measurement first.
- **Procedure per item, as the owner set it on 11 November.** Contract tests with the previous-version contrast, the immutable pin from a clean checkout (the
  one step that protects old saves), and the item's own paired lab at the new version (12 seeds, 3 arms, 25 days, about 12 minutes). No twin (every item here
  changes the world from its first use, so the twin would only confirm that). No second lab at the previous version: the lab of the item before is the baseline,
  compared with `scripts/lab_versions.ts` as before; the one baseline run is rules 196 once more at step 0, because the new tally metrics are not in the
  existing 196 rows. The full suite runs once, at the end of all the items, from a clean checkout; typecheck runs per item. So an item costs about 15 minutes
  of machine time, the plan about three hours, with a census re-run (`census.ts`, 6 minutes) after every third item.

## The items

### 1. Approaches for company stop failing on a coin and being dropped mid-walk (rules 200)

- **Problem.** A third of approaches fail before any talk; 16 percent of all approaches end "was too busy to stop" and 7 percent "something better came up"
  (`census/approach-*.json`). Children lose most: 259 of 425 foraging-practice approaches and 159 of 255 play approaches ended "too busy to stop".
- **Mechanism.** `social.ts acceptsTalk`: the approached person stops with probability `avail x (0.5 + 0.45 x sociability) + affinity / 220 + urgency + 0.2 if kin`;
  `avail` is the current activity's declared availability (gathering 0.45, building 0.35 to 0.4, farming 0.3, production 0.2, a meal 0.1, rest 0.9, wander 1,
  sleep and time alone 0); urgency is 0.4 for a warning, 0.12 for a request, offer or invitation, 0 otherwise. A refusal sets a 120-tick pair cooldown. The asker's approach
  (`options_social.ts mkSocial`) is an ordinary activity with `minCommit` 40 and `maxTicks` 420, so the review can drop it on its walk.
- **Change.** `src/sim/approach_rules.ts` (switch `setApproachRules`), asked by `acceptsTalk` and `mkSocial`: (a) childhood and learning purposes (`family_time`,
  `play_together`, `learn_foraging`, `learn_skill`, `learn_technique`) carry the request's urgency, 0.12, so a child's ask is as hard to put off as a request for
  goods; (b) the approach is committed for its walk, `minCommit` = the walk's estimate plus 20, capped at 240, so only danger or a critical need ends it before
  the answer. Nothing else: the roll, the availabilities, the 120-tick cooldown, the refusals for avoidance, pressing needs and sleep stay. The availabilities
  themselves (a gatherer stops 45 percent of the time at most) are the second lever if the first is not enough, measured separately.
- **Tests.** Staged: a child's practice ask to a gatherer is accepted at 200 on a roll that 199 refused (the 0.12); a request's own acceptance is unchanged; an
  approach three tiles out survives a review at 200 and is dropped at 199; a sleeper still refuses; danger still ends the approach; switch off and authored
  world keep 199.
- **Pin.** Normal gen-2, arrivals on, to the first tick a childhood approach is accepted on a roll 199 would have refused (sampled every tick), plus 120 after a
  save and load.
- **Measure.** Approach failure share (add it to `LifeTally`: socialize ends against converse successes), foraging practice, child play, family time, chats,
  coaching. Expected: failure share from 0.34 toward 0.25; practice and play up beyond their floors (±10.8, ±9.1).
- **Risk.** More talk means less gathering: watch the work share, tools and solid homes; the pin for 195 shows the work share rose with the free time, so a fall
  back toward 0.13 would be the signal to stop.

### 2. The settlement's first timber yard is a communal workplace (rules 201)

- **Problem.** About 8,100 blocked decisions per settlement want fibre, net, garment, blanket or trap work and "do not know of a timber yard they may use"
  (`summary.txt`). The yard (1.0 per settlement, built on day 9 to 11) belongs to the household that built it; `facilities.ts accessLevel` gives everyone else
  `none` unless they built a fifth of it or are a friend of a member with affinity 12. Nets, traps (hunting ended in meat 0.1 times), wool garments and sheep (0)
  all sit behind it.
- **Mechanism.** `proposeProject` (`townships.ts`) already makes a communal site when `communal && mayShareType(w, site.type) && site.hhId === 0` (shared builders,
  rules 186), and a communal building is `hhId 0`, access `communal`. The first storehouse goes this way since rules 192 (`shared_stone.ts firstStorehouseWanted`).
- **Change.** `src/sim/shared_yard.ts` (switch `setSharedYard`): the first timber yard a settlement lays out is proposed as a communal project the way the first
  storehouse is, `hhId 0`, so `mayUse` is `communal` for everyone in the township; later yards stay private. The yard's stock rules for a communal building already
  exist (`houseStockOpen`). Design note first: who fuels and repairs a communal yard (the storehouse's answer, shared builders, applies), and whether the
  production planner's "a yard stands" clauses (`production.ts`) read a communal yard as satisfying the hall and bakery conditions (they read `world.buildings`, so
  yes).
- **Tests.** Staged: a person of another household is refused the yard at 200 ("not theirs to use") and operates at 201; the second yard stays private; the
  communal yard's planks are open stock; switch off and authored world keep 200.
- **Pin.** Normal river, arrivals on, to the first tick a non-owner operates the yard.
- **Measure.** `operate` successes by product (add fibre, net, garment, trap counts to `LifeTally` or read them from the census), hunts, traps, garments, sheep,
  cold exposure (garments), and the blocked count. Expected: the 8,100 blocks fall by an order of magnitude; nets and traps appear in some seeds; no claim on
  sheep inside 25 days.
- **Risk.** A communal yard's planks are open to all, so the owner's own plank plans compete; the earmark system (`facilities.ts liveEarmarks`) already protects
  spoken-for stock.

### 3. Shared meals happen (rules 202)

- **Problem.** 0.3 meals hosted per settlement in 25 days; 22 invitations offered, 1.4 chosen.
- **Mechanism.** `meals.ts mealOptions`: the invitation wants hunger, thirst and energy drives under 24, 24 and 40; a cooldown; the hosting window (day fraction
  0.50 to 0.68); `spareFood` of 3 beyond 2 per dependant, counted from the pack only; a place (hall or fire); a friend of affinity 6 (or a housemate) within 14
  tiles who is awake and not talking; a score of 7 from sociability, generosity and distance.
- **Change.** `src/sim/meal_reach.ts` (switch `setMealReach`): (a) spare food counts the believed stock of the host's own home store (the table already eats from
  the store since rules 9, `tableItems`), less the household's keep; (b) the window opens at 0.35 (midday); (c) the guest radius 14 becomes the sight radius the
  host already has (`ctx.seenPersons`, up to 9 tiles, so no change) plus people they know to be at home within 20 tiles (`whereIs`), which is the knowledge they
  have. Nothing else: the score, the friend threshold, the cooldown.
- **Tests.** Staged: a host with an empty pack and 6 food at home is offered the invitation at 202 and not at 201; the window at 0.4 at 202 and not at 201; the
  score unchanged; switch off and authored keep 201.
- **Pin.** Normal meadow, arrivals on, to the first store-backed invitation.
- **Measure.** `mealsSat`, invitations chosen, supper practices (`supperHoldersAtEnd`, `familySupperAtEnd`, both 0 now), meeting knowledge. Expected: meals from 0.3
  to one or more a week per settlement; the supper tradition appearing in some seeds.
- **Risk.** Store food eaten at tables competes with dependants; the keep stays, and `DAY_FOOD` reserves are untouched.

### 4. The market is held at the fire when there is no hall (rules 203)

- **Problem.** No hall in 36 of 36 arms over 25 days, so no market (`occasionsTick` requires a hall) and no brewing. The hall's own gate (`production.ts`) needs
  three solid homes known and a yard or 24 people, outside a shortage, under the project cap, on a hash; at 195 the crowd clause is met around day 20 and the one
  hall site seen was starved of stone.
- **Mechanism.** `occasions.ts callOccasion` already falls back from the hall to the fire for every occasion; only the market's trigger insists on a hall.
- **Change.** `src/sim/market_fire.ts` (switch `setMarketFire`): the market is called at the fire on its day when there is no hall, once the settlement has four
  households and the day is 6 or later; `marketSwap` and `marketExchange` run as they do. The hall keeps its own gate; a hall, when it comes, takes the market
  back. Second look, separately: the hall's stone cost against the settlement's stone production (rules 162).
- **Tests.** Staged: a fire and four households call a market on day 7 at 203 and not at 202; with a hall the market is at the hall; three households call none.
- **Pin.** Normal aspen, arrivals on, to the first market at the fire.
- **Measure.** Occasions by kind (add market to the census), trade conversations, `retainedExchangePairs`/`Receipts` (0 now), attendances. Expected: markets 2 to
  3 per settlement; trade conversations from 6 upward; no claim on brewing (it still wants a hall).
- **Risk.** A market at the fire draws people from gathering on its day; attendance is already bounded (`MEET_AT_MOST`).

### 5. A site that has waited provokes the ask that unstalls it (rules 204)

- **Problem.** About 11,900 decisions per settlement found a site "waiting for materials" (stone 5,372, planks 2,556, bricks 1,374); "ask somebody to help with the
  hut" was chosen 7 times in 366 offers; project asks 2.3 per settlement; hut sites abandoned 0.8 per settlement.
- **Mechanism.** The help ask (`options_social.ts optRecruit`, `canInvite`, the recruit cap of rules 172) scores from the asker's traits and distance and is outranked
  by another solo stone trip. The site does not record how long it has waited.
- **Change.** Design note first (it may need a durable field). Preferred: no new field; the asker's own belief of the site carries `seen` and the need, and the
  ask's score rises with the age of a belief whose need has not fallen since it was last seen (`beliefAge`), by up to the value of a stone trip. Fallback: a
  `lastProgress` tick on the site (save-format note). `src/sim/stalled_ask.ts`, switch `setStalledAsk`.
- **Tests.** Staged: a site unchanged for a day lifts the ask above a stone trip at 204 and not at 203; a site that progressed yesterday does not; the recruit cap
  and `canInvite` unchanged.
- **Pin.** Normal birch, arrivals on, to the first lifted ask.
- **Measure.** `projectAsks`, `askUnitsDelivered`, `solidHomes`, abandoned sites (add to the census), `recruit` conversations. Expected: asks from 2.3 to a
  handful per settlement; abandoned sites down; stone deliveries do not rise by themselves (stone is production-limited).
- **Risk.** Asking does not make stone; the ask may be refused for want of it. The measure that matters is sites finished, not asks made.

### 6. The coaching and practice gate follows the drive, not 65 (rules 205)

- **Problem.** Coaching asks offered about 150 times and chosen 9; apprentices 0.3; coaching approaches failed 9 of 9. The learner's side
  (`options_social.ts`, line 739) wants hunger, thirst and energy all above 65; the fed window is 18 percent of awake time at 195. Childhood time wants 42.
- **Change.** `src/sim/learning_gate.ts` (switch `setLearningGate`): the coaching and foraging-practice gate reads "no drive pressing" (`drives` all 0, which is
  hunger above 62, thirst above 66, energy above 46) instead of the flat 65. The teacher's side (two workers of the skill within ten tiles, a master at 1.2 with a
  lead of 0.15, the offer hash) is unchanged in this version and measured; if coaching asks succeed but apprenticeships do not follow, the teacher's gate is the
  next version.
- **Tests.** Staged: a learner at hunger 63, thirst 67, energy 50 is offered coaching at 205 and not at 204; at hunger 60 neither; switch and authored.
- **Pin.** Normal gen-4, arrivals on, to the first coaching ask below 65.
- **Measure.** `coaching` conversations, apprentices (add to `LifeTally`), techniques learned, `foragingPractice`. Expected: coaching from 2.9 toward the floor's
  edge (±2.0) or beyond; apprentices from 0.3 upward.
- **Risk.** None to survival: the drive gate is above the drive's start by construction.

### 7. A swap that feeds somebody now is scored by the trip it saves (rules 206)

- **Problem.** Swap offers appear about a thousand times per settlement and are chosen about twenty; trade conversations 6.
- **Mechanism.** `options_social.ts optTrade` scores a swap from the want and the counterpart's goods, below a gathering trip even when the want is food and the
  gather is two hours away.
- **Change.** `src/sim/swap_worth.ts` (switch `setSwapWorth`): when the want is food and the hunger drive is above 0, the swap's score adds the relief the gather
  would cost, read from `relief.ts` (the eta to the nearest believed food), bounded by the eat option's own weight; non-food swaps unchanged.
- **Tests.** Staged: a hungry person with wood beside a friend with berries, nearest bush 40 tiles off, ranks the swap above the bush at 206 and below at 205; with
  the bush five tiles off the gather still wins; switch and authored.
- **Pin.** Normal cedar, arrivals on, to the first swap chosen by relief.
- **Measure.** `trade` conversations, `otherTalk`, `meanHunger`, hunger exposure. Expected: trade conversations from 6 toward 20; nothing on deaths.
- **Risk.** Swaps move food between households; conservation is through the ledger already.

### 8. The fire is kept for the gathering (rules 207)

- **Problem.** The communal fire went out 23 times per settlement; 3,347 blocked warmings "thinks the fire has gone out"; every meal and occasion is held at the
  fire; "keep the fire going" as a responsibility was offered 17 times and taken 0.7.
- **Mechanism.** `options_work.ts` fuel option: offered when fuel is believed under 150, or at night, in rain, or on a promise; one wood is 380 fuel; wood in
  hand is the limit (898 "no room in my pack for one wood").
- **Change.** `src/sim/fire_for_the_gathering.ts` (switch `setFireForGathering`): when an occasion or meal is called at a fire for today, the fuel option is offered
  from 400 fuel believed and weighted by the gathering, and the host or the occasion's first attendee is offered the "keep the fire going" responsibility.
- **Tests.** Staged: a called feast lifts the fuel option at 207 and not at 206; no gathering, no change; the responsibility offered to the host.
- **Pin.** Normal fern, arrivals on, to the first gathering-weighted refuel.
- **Measure.** Fire-out events (add to `LifeTally` from the event text), attendances, `mealsSat`, cold exposure. Expected: fire-outs from 23 toward 15; attendance
  up inside its floor.
- **Risk.** Wood diverted from building; small, and the option still needs wood in hand.

### 9. Remembered emptiness: measure before any change (no rules version yet)

- **Problem as it looked.** Half of all blocked decisions, 72,000 per settlement, are a remembered emptiness: berries 40,275, the home store 21,633, grain 10,778.
- **What the code already does.** `knowledge.ts estimatedAmount` already turns a stale empty memory hopeful at the source's own regrowth rate (a berry unit every
  300 ticks, slower on worn ground), so most of those berry blocks are sightings less than a few hours old of bushes that really were bare: real scarcity, not a
  knowledge fault. A store, though, never "regrows" in memory: a store seen empty stays empty in the belief until the next sight, however much the household
  brings home.
- **Plan.** A probe first (one session): for a sample of "believes it is empty" and "remembers it being empty" blocks, compare the belief with the truth at that
  tick, by kind. If the store half is mostly wrong (food came home unseen), the change is a knowledge rule: a household member's own deposit, or the sight of a
  member walking home with food, updates the believer's store glance. If the bush half is mostly right, nothing changes and the record says so.
