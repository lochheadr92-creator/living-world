# Completed building shares and bounded promise renegotiation — rules 85

## Reproduction and contract

The explicit rules-84 diagnostic (`scripts/promise_delay_diagnostic.ts`,
`autonomy-delay85-before.log`) records a real accepted watch request and a declared impassable
strip. The helper's actual approach fails at tick 324. At a physical requester rendezvous,
tick 526, the helper has no critical need, the child is alive, the promise remains active
until 2440, and no delay explanation is offered. The requester knows only the original
agreement. This establishes a missing speech transition, not that more time solves the route.

Rules 85 allows the helper to explain a matching, still-unresolved failed attempt and ask for
one additional six-hour window. The existing option and conversation systems own approach,
eligibility, interruption and speech. The requester hears the actual attempted activity,
reported partial quantities/work and proposed time, without firsthand work credit or assumed
intent. The helper's original obligation remains active. Hearing alone does not change either
agreed deadline, transfer goods or repair the obstruction.

During the actual answer the requester can accept or refuse from their own urgency and trust.
A personal hunger, thirst or warmth value below 35, urgent care, a personally remembered roof below 25
condition, trust below 10, an ended agreement or an elapsed proposal prevents assent. A
six-hour extension is unsuitable for the current watch/fire request; refusing time does not
silently cancel the existing duty. Other eligible requesters may grant the bounded time.
Acceptance updates the helper's deadline, the requester's heard expectation and the matching
shared request together. A personally timed-out expectation may be explicitly renewed by that
answer; other terminal expectations are not revived. Survival grace remains private and does
not become a secretly communicated new deadline.

There is at most one proposal per existing obligation. An interrupted explanation can be
repeated after 400 ticks, with the same proposed deadline. An answered proposal cannot keep
extending itself. Corrected attempts, actual progress beyond that failed attempt, ended
obligations, personally known death and expired proposal windows prevent a fresh offer.
The original heard deadline and its conversation remain distinct from the later answer.

## Ownership, persistence and limits

`src/sim/promise_delays.ts` owns the bounded proposal, contact transitions, personal answer
rule, explanation and validation. The existing commitment owns the helper's copy; the existing
32-entry requester memory owns the reported copy inside its heard expectation. A delay proposal creates no helper
terminal receipt. `options_social.ts` offers at most two eligible nearby
conversations. `social.ts` executes the ask and answer; `promise_expectations.ts` and
`inspect_work.ts` display their distinct provenance. `types.ts`, `save.ts` and `world.ts`
carry, validate and hash the new records. Lab removal: `rich-no-promise-delays`. Rules 84
was pinned at `eba0b94` and published at `e4f5c03` before this behavior.

This is failed-attempt delay communication with one bounded time negotiation. It does not
supply an alternative route, guarantee successful retry, grant arbitrary extensions, search
for distant requesters or finish general quantity/place counteroffers and ability questions.
A later different failure after an answered proposal does not open another extension; terminal
outcomes still use the existing report path. Wider cooperation and the full programme remain
open. No survival, productivity or population benefit is claimed.
