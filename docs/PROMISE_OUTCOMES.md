## Ownership and contract

`src/sim/promise_outcomes.ts` owns one latest execution attempt per existing commitment and
outcome metadata inside the existing 32-entry personal request memory. There is no new queue
or general memory store. `activities.ts` records actual failed/partial registered execution
after the handler credits its physical contribution. Explicit commitment IDs link delivery,
watch and return activities; exact repair-building and help-build-site matches cover work
whose registered action has no commitment ID. Unrelated resource gathering is not guessed
to belong to an obligation. This remaining acquisition-attribution gap is explicit.

An attempt records activity, kind, target, travel/work phase, tick, bounded reason (240
characters), and actual contribution/delivery counters. Validated later arrival corrects a
matching failed approach; it cannot correct a work failure. Successful matching work and
subsequent contribution also prevent an obsolete attempt from explaining a later miss.

At the existing effective deadline, full completion retains rules-80 behavior. Otherwise:

- Actual partial work expires with its measured percentage.
- A recent own-needs interruption is recorded as interrupted.
- A retained unresolved execution failure or recent local feasibility blocker yields failed.
- Missing cause evidence yields expired, with cause not established. It does not establish
  deliberate breach from the mere absence of a recent blocker.

No requester relationship, grievance, firsthand story, last-met timestamp or receipt is
manufactured by this deadline path. Even proximity at expiry does not prove the requester
witnessed the earlier cause. Positive direct completion, legitimate refusal, theft and
other established dispute paths are unchanged. This does not yet implement a requester's
personal remembered deadline or their own recognition that a delivery has failed to arrive.

The helper's terminal outcome survives pruning of operational requests/commitments in the
bounded request memory. Within 12 days, it can offer an ordinary reporting trip to an eligible
visible recipient. At most two eligible reports are offered. Actual conversation contact is
required for speech and acknowledgement; a sleeping or separate-room listener cannot receive
it. The recipient records teller, conversation, outcome time and reported attempt provenance,
without inheriting firsthand task receipts, competence evidence or proof of intent. Repeated
hearing cannot multiply the retained event or an outcome-specific relationship reward. A
400-tick retry delay lives in the same bounded memory; acknowledgement clears it. Physical
conversation's ordinary time/needs costs and interruption remain in force.

Save validation rejects unsupported versions, future times, inconsistent roles, false
firsthand receipts, resolved attempts presented as unresolved explanations and invalid retry
bounds. Attempts participate in the deterministic hash; existing personal memories already
do. History renders the source, attempt and acknowledgement without mutating the world.
Isolated removal: `rich-no-promise-outcomes`. Earlier rules are unchanged.
