## Ownership and behavior

`src/sim/promise_expectations.ts` owns the requester's heard agreement inside the existing
32-entry request memory. It is not another general memory store. The actual answer phase
records the commitment and conversation IDs, agreed amount/item, plan/place, identity and a
spoken expected time. The speaker rounds the existing execution deadline up to a whole hour
and says the resulting number of hours. The requester's waiting period uses exactly that
communicated horizon. The helper's later private sleep/survival grace does not silently extend
what the requester expects. Delay negotiation remains separate unfinished work.

The requester uses this record for outstanding material amounts, own-plan help, repeated
watch/fire asking, request inspection and restricted AI observation. A shared engine request
or another person's commitment is no longer private progress knowledge. Actual witnessed
hand-over credits the known quantity; a sleeping recipient does not learn who brought it
through this callback. Actual observed completion or a spoken report closes the expectation.
A late report can revise an earlier personal timeout without duplicating the agreement or
creating firsthand competence evidence. The original heard agreement remains attached.

When the requester’s communicated waiting period ends, their reason is that no completion is
known and the cause is unknown. This does not assert that work never happened or establish bad
faith. Learning a helper's death, or the child's death for an agreed watch, releases the
expectation through the existing witness/grave/told knowledge. No truth-detection mechanism
is added. Conflicting death reports and belief revision still need broader work.

Death cleanup still immediately removes the dead person and releases their physical claims,
loans and belongings. A living distant helper's personal obligation survives until they learn
the death or reach their own effective deadline. At that deadline their own failed/partial/
interrupted/unknown outcome can be retained even when the engine's shared request is already
cancelled. That private record does not rewrite the lifecycle's shared cancellation. A known
death ends the relevant active journey and releases its claims.

Registered watch and material-delivery options use the accepted identity and the helper's
own seen/remembered position. An unseen death cannot remove the trip through a global living
person lookup. Identity labels may resolve the already-known ID through the historical name
registry; no life state or current position is disclosed by that lookup. At arrival, an absent
recipient is “not where I expected,” old whereabouts are cleared, and the watch retry remains
bounded. Reading a grave during travel can legitimately close the obligation before arrival.
Tool-return ownership changes and unrelated care/search readers are not declared repaired by
this slice.

Save validation checks the bounded agreement, IDs, quantities, source, timestamps and outcome
consistency. Existing personal-memory hashing covers every new field. Pure request/history
readers do not mutate state. `rich-no-promise-expectations` restores the previous reader and
death-cancellation behavior; earlier rules and their recorded continuations remain unchanged.
