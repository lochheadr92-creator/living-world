## Connected behavior and ownership

`src/sim/promise_targets.ts` owns one optional local target-evidence record in each existing
commitment. It records target identity/kind, tick, coordinates and either observed absence or
completion by this worker. It is not a second general memory store. `refreshBeliefs` and actual
repair/build/fire arrival/work are local observation boundaries; the worker who physically
completes a site records that distinct event. A distant registry deletion and a forgotten
belief are not evidence. Sleeping people and people in a different interior do not acquire
these outdoor observations.

Commitment review preserves unknown distant targets until personal evidence or the helper's
own effective deadline. Unknown site disappearance no longer means the building was completed.
Observed absence cancels an impossible obligation and releases its matching action/claims,
retaining actual partial quantities and work. The reason explicitly leaves the site's fate
unknown; it asserts neither intentional abandonment nor another person's wrongdoing. A worker
who actually completes a work promise, or has already delivered the agreed haul amount, retains
that completed contribution. Finishing construction does not fulfill an outstanding material
promise: that record ends as a cancellation with its real delivered amount and an explanation
that the completed site can no longer receive the remainder. Work is described as work, not a fictional zero-item delivery.

Actual building recruitment now attaches the same heard agreement and spoken time already used
for other requests, at the accepted conversation boundary. No injected acceptance or remote
assignment is introduced. Local cancellation is retained in the existing bounded request receipt,
then offered through the existing explanation conversation, heard by a present requester and
acknowledged once. Its source remains the helper's account, not the requester's firsthand proof
of loss or competence. An unaware requester retains their original expectation until contact
or their own waiting period ends.

A review finding exposed fire credit arriving too late: fuel was recorded only at activity end.
Rules 83 records the tick at actual wood consumption, before any subsequent target loss. Own
performed fire duty is settled before target cancellation. Actual fuel and resource consumption
remain authoritative; no work credit is created by the promise or its report.

Existing owners touched: perception and action handlers capture local evidence; commitment
upkeep settles it; request memory/outcome reporting retains and communicates it; history displays
source and uncertainty; save validation checks evidence; full-state hashing includes it. Lab
removal `rich-no-promise-targets` restores prior target-loss inference. Earlier rules retain their
recorded continuations. Group/helper caps, recruitment's other global eligibility readers,
requester-initiated cancellation/delay, informed target repair by someone else, general dispute
resolution and broader household rebuilding remain open programme work.
