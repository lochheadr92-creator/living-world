# Wolf defence (rules version 3 on this branch)

This is the independent `claude/wolf-defence-v3` line, cut from `main` at `1a13408` before main's
habitation and wolf-integration work existed. **Rules version 3 on THIS branch means the spear and
the active defence, and nothing else** - no habitation, no indoor refuge. Main later used the same
number for different rules; the collision is written down at the bottom of this file, not resolved
by force.

## What version 3 means here

- `a5157f8` a spear: an ordinary tool, wanted only by somebody who has met a wolf.
- `b204cb7` an armed, fit adult who can SEE a wolf close to themselves or to a visible neighbour
  may walk over and try to drive it off: walk to REACH, face it, spend ATTEMPT_TICKS jabbing,
  settle it by hash of seed, pair and tick. Success sends the wolf into retreat, wary of that
  person for a bounded while. The wolf is never hurt. A failure is a real failure: nothing
  credited, defender left two tiles from a wolf that is still stalking.
- `a99fbfd` the second pass, which makes the first real. Forty staged encounters had shown a wolf
  closes the last tile in about seven ticks and bites at 1.2 - every staged defender was bitten
  before she could try. Two changes: the defender holds her ground when the wolf is coming to
  her (the spot of the option is her own place, not the wolf's), and the wolf hesitates before
  somebody squared up to it - spear in hand, attempt under way and in its working phase - for at
  most the length of one short attempt (ATTEMPT_TICKS is 10, measured rather than chosen). A
  standoff with an outcome, not protection: a failed attempt leaves her standing beside a wolf
  that is free to bite again.

## The before-the-change reference

`b0dd770`, recorded at `1a13408` before version 3 existed: a version-2 continuation reference
with the new instruments copied in. Two version-2 cases, including `v2-wolves` (fern, to tick
9600) - a world with wolves roaming in it, because wolves are what version 3 answers. Three
counts (spears in existence, wolves driven off, what the wolves are doing) are asserted zero in
every case below version 3. The version-1 cases were extended with the new counts and every
existing value came back identical. The full suite passes against it, so no version-3 behaviour
leaked into a version-1 or version-2 world.

## Staged and autonomous evidence

`encounters_out/report.md`, regenerated by `scripts/encounters.ts` (40 seeds, 900 ticks each,
three arms per seed: action on, action off with the same spear, unarmed):

- Defence on: 40 set out, 40 reached the wolf and tried, 18 drove it off, 0.55 bites mean.
- Action off (the `rich-no-defence` ablation: same spear, she just will not use it): 0 driven
  off, 1 bite. Unarmed: 0 driven off, 1 bite.
- An ordinary village left to itself (28 people, 12 days): one spear made, zero chances to
  stand, zero bites. With every adult armed on day 2: 52 chances, 7 set out, 3 reached and
  tried, 3 drove off, 1 death.

This is mechanism evidence - the action runs and costs what it costs - never a survival benefit.
Nothing in it is a village.

## The version-number collision with main (recorded, not forced)

Main moved past this line. As of `b050816` (the product brief) main's numbers mean:

| number | main | this branch |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | habitation (`docs/HABITATION.md`) | wolf defence (this file) |
| 4 | wolf defence integrated with habitation and home refuge (`docs/WOLF_DEFENCE.md`) | not issued |
| 6 | fatal-injury finality | not issued |

Main's `docs/WOLF_DEFENCE.md` states this line's version-3 saves are **not compatible** with its
number meanings. Same number, different rules: a save is meaningless without its source
revision, no automatic conversion is claimed, and no provenance is inferred from an unmarked
save.

Not done here: re-issuing the number on this branch. Renumbering would break this line's own
version-2-to-3 continuity record and its save and replay semantics for a save that never
shipped. The collision is written down on both sides; reconciling the two lines, if the owner
chooses to, must be a separately negotiated change, not a side effect of landing the feature.
